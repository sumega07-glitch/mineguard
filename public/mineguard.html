❓ How to Demo MineGuard

PRD Evaluation Test Scenarios

  1. Normal Readings: Click "Normal (25/60)" preset. All 4 zones show green Normal status; no alerts created.
  2. Warning PM2.5: Click "PM2.5 Warn (75)" preset. Crushing area flags Warning; yellow alert logged with misting advice.
  3. Critical PM2.5: Click "PM2.5 Crit (125)" preset. Mining area crosses 100 limit; Critical alert triggered.
  4. Critical PM10: Click "PM10 Crit (230)" preset. Transportation haul road reports severe dust; highest bar in comparison chart.
  5. Boundary Condition: Click "Boundary (60/120)" preset. Verifies >= threshold rule.
  6. Threshold Settings: Open Demo Thresholds. Enter Warning higher than Critical to see validation rejection.
